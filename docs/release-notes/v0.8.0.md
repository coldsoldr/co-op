# co-op V0.8.0

Released 2026-10-30.

## Audition patches on your OP-1

Hear a patch on the OP-1 itself, without disk mode and without copying anything first.

- **Click a patch to load it.** The row fills from left to right as the patch transfers, and
  clicking another patch cancels the transfer.
- **Your sound is safe.** The patch only lives in memory, and **Put my sound back** restores your
  slot.
- **Works with most patch types:**

| Patch | Load time |
| --- | --- |
| Synth engines | Instant |
| Dbox kits | Instant |
| Samplers | Up to about 20 seconds |
| Sampled drum kits | Up to about 40 seconds |

- **Requirements:** hundekraft 3.0.3, the new `audition` plugin, and the OP-1 plugged in normally,
  not in disk mode.

## New Patches tab

Browse patches with nothing plugged in.

- **Your own folder:** point co-op at a folder of patches on your computer, in any layout, and it
  remembers it. This works in Chrome, Edge and Vivaldi.
- **Community patches:** every patch from the community packs, in one list.
- **Filters:** search by name, or filter by kind, engine, and folder or pack.
- **Preview in the browser:** a preview button on each row, plus a 24-key keyboard you can play
  with the mouse or computer keys. Drum keys play their own slices, and samplers are repitched from
  their root note.
- **No more voice clip:** patches that only hold the OP-1's spoken "patch" clip now show
  **no preview**.

## Library

- **Local folders:** your folder appears under **local**, next to synth and drum, stacked like a
  file manager.
- **Send to OP-1:** tick one or many patches and send them to the folders you choose.
- **Works without a drive:** with the OP-1 out of disk mode, you can still browse your local folder
  and audition from it. Synth patches and dbox kits from the drive also still load.

## Plugins

- **Grouped by author,** with collapsible groups.
- **Try on my OP-1:** install a plugin, try it, then keep or remove it.
- **Overwrite from co-op:** if a plugin is already installed, co-op compares version and size and
  can replace it in one restart.
- **New `audition` plugin:** version 0.7 is now in the library, with its source published.
- **Demo videos:** plugin cards can now show one, though none have been added yet.

## Devices

- **Device menu:** click the logo to switch between OP-1, OP-XY and OP-1F (coming soon).
- **OP-XY design preview:** the new page is a design preview using sample data. It can't connect to
  an OP-XY yet.

## Fixes and polish

- The `user` folder's row no longer changes height when selected.
- Download and Install buttons on plugin cards are now properly spaced.

## Known limitations

- Gin kits can't audition on the OP-1.
- Sampler patches and sampled kits on the OP-1 drive can't load outside disk mode. Audition them
  from your local folder.
- A sampler or kit slot sounds scrambled while it loads.
- Turning a knob during an audition lets the OP-1 save that sound over the slot.
- Auditioning on the OP-1 needs hundekraft 3.0.3. It isn't available on stock firmware, and opus
  hasn't been looked at yet.
