# Pacing: 600 frames, then a screenshot every 40 frames (~2-3 s of guest time in op1emu). Steps (guest ms):
#  30000 settings (the four mode keys), right: VOICES, blue 4 detents: "channel strip", green right: ON, left left
#  41000 mixer (T1)
#  45000 blue push: the strip of tape 1, EQ. White +6 detents (band 1 +3 dB); blue: band 2; white -4 (-2 dB);
#        green +3 (freq); orange +2 (Q); blue push on EQ: the EQ on
#  54000 green push: COMP; green push: comp on; blue +10 (thresh -23 dB); green +1 (ratio); white +2 (attack);
#        shift: white +2 (release), orange +2 (knee); orange +4 (makeup +2 dB)
#  63000 white push: SAT; white push: sat on; blue +10 (drive 40); green +1 (tube); white +5 (tone); orange -10 (mix 90)
#  70000 orange push: SEND; green +50 (FX 1 50 %); blue -20 (dry 80 %); orange -1 (tap pre)
#  76000 shift + orange push: tap back to its default (post)
#  77000 T2: tape 2's strip (SEND)
#  81000 T1; orange push: the sends bypassed; 82500 orange push: back
#  84000 the mixer key: closed (the mixer's T1 page)
#  88000 T2: the master EQ page with the note; 92000 T4: master comp with the note; 96000 T1
# Expected (g_sui, g_sui_p[0] dumped at exit): opens 1, closes 1, pages 3, bypasses 5, resets 1, tapes 2;
# track 1: eq_on 1, band 1 gain 42, band 2 gain 32 freq 63 q 9; comp_on 1 thresh 46 ratio 5 attack 17 release 18
