# Stereo tape loop: record a chord (tape 0.8-1.8 s), STOP x2 (rewind), tape page, loop in at 0 (sound key 1 =
# key code 0x2d on the tape page, docs/tape-stereo.md section 11), PLAY 1.5 s, STOP, loop out there (sound key 2 =
# 0x2e; the keys turn the loop on), STOP x2 (rewind), PLAY 8.5 s: the chord should repeat about every 1.5 s with
# its stereo width and no click at the seam.
accel 64 -64 512
wait-frames 1 300000
at 30000
tap synth 300
at 32000
press rec
at 32200
tap play 300
at 32600
release rec
at 33000
press c4
press e4
press g4

tap tape 300
at 39000
