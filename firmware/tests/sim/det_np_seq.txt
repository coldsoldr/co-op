# e16 nophysics (docs/nophysics.md): the synth sequencer after boot, as the saved setting leaves it. Boot, synth,
# the seq key, a chord through the sequencer, nothing else: the screenshots (wall-paced `wait`) show whichever
# sequencer the setting resolved to (stock with a saved Tombola: Tombola; nophysics: pattern). Run after
# det_np_pick.txt on the same NAND (`--chain`, or tools that keep the NAND) for the saved-state check.
accel 64 -64 512
wait-frames 1 300000
at 30000
tap synth 300
at 32000
tap seq 300
at 34000
press c4
press e4
press g4
at 36000
release c4
release e4
release g4
wait 33000
screenshot /root/op1emu-work/np_seq_a.ppm
wait 4000
screenshot /root/op1emu-work/np_seq_b.ppm
wait 4000
screenshot /root/op1emu-work/np_seq_c.ppm
wait 4000
quit
