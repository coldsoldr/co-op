# Deterministic tape playback: tape screen, play from the current position, stop. Audio appears only if
# the NAND's tape has recorded content (the factory tape ships with a demo song).
accel 64 -64 512
wait-frames 1 300000
at 30000
tap tape 300
at 31000
tap play 300
at 40000
tap stop 300
wait 50000
quit
