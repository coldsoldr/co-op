# mouse

The driver for a USB mouse plugged into the OP-1 with a mini USB OTG adapter. An arrow on the screen takes the colour of the encoder it stands on: the wheel or a drag up and down turns that encoder, a click is its push. In Opus's menu it picks the rows. On the tape a click moves the playhead and a drag scrubs, the loop's marks and the clips are dragged where they are drawn (above, a clip on its way to another place and track), and the wheel zooms. On matrix's page each value is under the arrow where it is drawn.

## Get it

mouse 1.2.1 is a plugin for Opus 2.0 beta 5, LizardMan's mod for the original OP-1 (firmware 246): one file in the starter pack, or on its own, in [Opus's releases](https://github.com/LizardMan245/opus/releases). Copy the `.opmod` into the mods folder on the OP-1's disk. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy. Opus's plugins are © LizardMan, free to use and share.
