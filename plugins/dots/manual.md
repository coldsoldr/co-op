# Dots

Six slots of up to 64 steps each, all playing at once at 1/16. Every step can have a note, a level, ratchets, a chance, a condition, a nudge and up to four effect locks.

## Who made it

From op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Dots is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
