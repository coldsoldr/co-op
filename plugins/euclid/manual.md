# Euclid

Euclid and Dots come from the op1r project. Pick one with Shift + seq. Synth and drums each have their own sequencer, and both follow the OP-1's tempo and play the current synth or drum kit. The keys they share are in the next section.

## Who made it

From op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Euclid is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
