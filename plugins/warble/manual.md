# Warble

*Chorus, vibrato and tape wobble, after Maiko.*

A short delay whose time wobbles in two uneven rhythms, so the movement never repeats exactly. Half mix gives chorus; full mix gives vibrato. A slow rate and small depth give tape-like pitch movement. The cassette artwork comes from Maiko's #250 with permission; its hubs turn and the loose tape moves with the controls.

## Who made it

Reconstructs Maiko Pruett's #250 algorithm and artwork, used with permission.

## Get it

Warble is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
