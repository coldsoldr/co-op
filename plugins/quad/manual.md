# Quad LFO

Four LFOs in one, each with its own target. They can modulate the engine encoders, the envelope, the effect, and each other. Works on synths and drums.

## Who made it

Lorenz waves and the Pond LFO by xoza; from op1r.

## Get it

Quad LFO is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
