# erdtree

*A small orchestra under the Erdtree.*

erdtree by SammiBabe666 is an Elden Ring tribute that plays like a small orchestra under the Erdtree. Each encoder brings in one section: blue a choir of spectral voices, green detuned violins and celli, orange Brassophone's horn, and white the distance between them. Each section also animates its own part of the screen: falling leaves, the crown, shafts of light and stars.

## Using it

Choose erdtree in synth mode (shift + T1, then erdtree in the engine list). On its T1 page:

- **Blue, choir:** harmonics 1 to 6 of a slightly sharp tone, with a slow breath and a shimmer on top. Leaves fall from the crown.
- **Green, strings:** two detuned violins and celli an octave down. The bow swells in, and vibrato arrives once the note settles. The crown grows and sways.
- **White, space:** wider detune, a darker tone, a slower bloom and a deeper breath. It isn't a reverb: for the cathedral, put one on the effect slot. Stars multiply.
- **Orange, brass:** louder and brighter with more, with a short lip scoop on each note. Its tone follows the T2 envelope. Shafts of light break under the crown.

Blue, green and orange are levels on a squared curve, so the first part of each turn fades a section in gently; with all three at zero there's silence. T2's envelope, T4's LFO and T3's effect are the OS's own.

## Who made it

erdtree is by SammiBabe666 ([Project_Erdtree](https://github.com/SammiBabe666/Project_Erdtree), GPL-3.0-or-later). The brass voice comes from Brassophone by nick (nvmsocool). Elden Ring and the Erdtree belong to FromSoftware and Bandai Namco: this is an unofficial fan tribute.

## Get it

There are two builds, each with its own presets, and they aren't interchangeable:

- **hundekraft:** erdtree.knl 0.5, for the Hundekraft Workshop 3.0. Install over USB from its card here (co-op fetches the file from the author's repo and checks it), or download `erdtree-hundekraft.zip` and follow its INSTALL.txt, which also brings the presets.
- **opus:** erdtree.opmod 0.6.1, for Opus core 2.8 or later. Download `erdtree-opus.zip` and follow its INSTALL.txt. The author notes 0.6.1 hasn't been confirmed on an OP-1 yet, so keep developer mode on while you try it, and its eight presets are in the preset guide to set by hand for now.

co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
