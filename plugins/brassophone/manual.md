# Brassophone

*A brass horn that leans into each note.*

Brassophone by nvmsocool starts each note as a soft sine and opens it into a brassy tone as the OP-1's envelope rises. The pitch follows the envelope too, up to about a quarter tone sharp at its peak, so notes lean in the way a horn player's do. Hold a note: once the envelope settles at sustain, vibrato fades in. Shape it on the normal T2 envelope page; a slow attack makes the swell easy to hear.

## Who made it

From op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Brassophone is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
