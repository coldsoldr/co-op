# Cathedral of the Hollow Saint

*A synth for hundekraft*

A haunted hurdy-gurdy for the original Teenage Engineering OP-1: a kennel synth plugin (hollow_saint) for the Hundekraft OP-1 Workshop 3.0.x. Bowed melody strings, a drone that can hold a key like real bourdons, a buzzing bridge that kicks in on tempo-locked wheel strokes, and a cathedral that darkens, rings and tolls a distant bell.

## Who made it

Cathedral of the Hollow Saint is by SammiBabe666 ([SammiBabe666/Project_Hollow_Saint](https://github.com/SammiBabe666/Project_Hollow_Saint), GPL-3.0-or-later).

## Get it

It needs hundekraft 3.0, on the original OP-1. Download it from [its page](https://github.com/SammiBabe666/Project_Hollow_Saint) and follow the install steps there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
