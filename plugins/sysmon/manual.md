# System monitor

Shift + help, then T1. The top shows CPU, audio load, memory, disk and MIDI activity. Press T1 again to close it. The AUDIO bar shows instrument, envelope, effect and other work, and its peak marker helps spot brief bursts of high load.

## Who made it

From op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

System monitor is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
