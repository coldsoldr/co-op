# SISTER

*A cathedral reverb whose tail sings, rings in tune or swells backwards.*

SISTER is Father's sister: a long stereo hall with three voices in its tail. Turn green toward the choir and the tail sings back what you played, on slowly drifting vowels (aah, ooh, eeh), like a choir far down the nave. At the other end it rings on organ pipes tuned to a root, its fifth and its octave, so drums and noise come back as a chord. White turns the tail around: each sound swells up backwards out of its own echo before it lands. Nothing switches modes. You can stop anywhere on a knob and it will sound like something.

## Get it

SISTER is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
