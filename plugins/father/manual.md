# Father

*A room that recedes into a long, moving tail.*

Father blends short, stereo-spread early reflections with a denser, gently moving stereo tail. Distance adds up to 30 ms of predelay; Gate starts off so the tail can decay naturally. Turn Distance up to move the sound farther away and lengthen the decay. The head with glasses drifts back as Distance rises. The small green person moves with Gate, and gate posts close around it when the tail is gated.

## Who made it

The starting settings are about 50 for Distance, Gate off, 54 for Color and 30 for Mix. If Father is already loaded, turn green to zero to hear the ungated tail. Only Distance has a large number; Mix uses the red bar on the right. Push white to switch CLASSIC on or off. Press T3 to bypass or enable it. FX OFF means it is bypassed.

## Get it

Father is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
