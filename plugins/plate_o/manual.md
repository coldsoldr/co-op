# plate_o

plate (Opus) is LizardMan's plate reverb for Opus, and it sits next to our Plate. On the OP-1 it's called plate_o. SIZE runs from a small room through chamber and plate to a hall, and the decay goes from 0.3 s to 20 s. The green lines on the screen are the tail.

## Get it

plate_o is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
