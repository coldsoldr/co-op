# Castle

*A four-operator FM synth with a castle to explore.*

Castle by displayname808 is a four-operator FM synth with a small world on its screen: a castle you explore while you play, whose villagers comment on every turn of a knob. It has eight algorithms and twenty ratio sets, a sine sub an octave down, saturation, noise, ring modulation and sample-rate reduction, and a drone mode. Turn orange up and the castle catches fire.

## Controls

On T1, turn an encoder, or hold shift and turn:

| Encoder | Turn | Shift + turn |
|---|---|---|
| Blue | FM: fades operators 2, 3 and 4 into operator 1 | The algorithm, one of eight |
| Green | The operator ratio set, one of twenty | Pitch modulation, up to 4 Hz and one semitone |
| White | Operator 4 feedback | Noise, and a mild sample-rate reduction |
| Orange | Saturation (and fire) | Ring modulation (the kaleidoscope moat) |

- Push white to show or hide the log, where the villagers talk.
- Push orange to switch drone mode on or off. With drone mode off, notes fade for three seconds after you let go.
- Castle sets its own level: the envelope and velocity don't change it. T2 lists the controls, and T4's LFOs can move the main knobs, the algorithm, the ring modulator and the reducer.

## Who made it

## Get it

Castle 1.0 runs on the original OP-1 with firmware 246 and OPUS core 2.29 or newer. Install OPUS first, then download `castle.opmod` from [Castle's page](https://displayname808hub.github.io/web/castle/), copy it into the OP-1's `mods` folder in disk mode, eject and restart. The mods list should say Castle is loaded; choose it as the synth engine. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
