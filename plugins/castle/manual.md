# castle

castle 1.1 is displayname808's FM drone synth. On screen is a castle on an island, with villagers going about their day and a log of what they get up to. It plays like any synth at first: a note sounds while you hold its key. Switch on drone mode and notes keep sounding after you let go.

## Get it

castle is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
