# Moai

*A Minimoog-style synth with 121 sounds.*

Moai by fungiboletus is modelled on the Minimoog Model D: fat basses, singing leads and brassy stabs, with 121 sounds to start from. Every voice has three oscillators and noise, a four-pole ladder filter with emphasis, drive and saturation, and a contour that sweeps the filter. Loudness comes from the OP-1's own envelope on T2, as on the other synths.

## Who made it

Moai is by fungiboletus ([op-moai](https://github.com/fungiboletus/op-moai), GPL-3.0-or-later). Kennel port by hundekraft.

## Get it

Moai is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.

On op1r 73 it is the plugin file `moai.op1p`: tick it in [op1r's web patcher](https://pcppcp.github.io/op1r/web/) (Connect OP-1 installs it over USB), or download it here with op1r chosen in Settings.
