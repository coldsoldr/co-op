# Moai

*A Minimoog-style synth with 121 sounds.*

Moai by fungiboletus is modelled on the Minimoog Model D: fat basses, singing leads and brassy stabs, with 121 sounds to start from. Every voice has three oscillators and noise, a four-pole ladder filter with emphasis, drive and saturation, and a contour that sweeps the filter. Loudness comes from the OP-1's own envelope on T2, as on the other synths.

## Who made it

Moai is by fungiboletus ([op-moai](https://github.com/fungiboletus/op-moai), GPL-3.0-or-later). Hundekraft brought it to the OP-1.

## Get it

Moai is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
