# In a room

*Every pass of the loop goes through a room.*

After Alvin Lucier's "I am sitting in a room" (1969). You record a phrase, and on every pass the loop plays through a model of a room and is recorded again. The room's resonances pile up, and after enough passes speech melts into a chord. The counter shows the generation. Choose one of four rooms with Shape. Push blue to set Loop to a free length or one of six tempo-synced lengths.

## Who made it

Musica Obscura by ultralazr, after Alvin Lucier; from op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

In a room is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
