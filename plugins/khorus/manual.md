# KHORUS

*A chorus you can see.*

KHORUS, by LizardMan, is a chorus with one to three voices and a triangle or sine sweep. It's stereo as the master effect. On the screen each voice is a stack of waves in its encoder's colour.

## Get it

KHORUS is part of hundekraft 3.3, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
