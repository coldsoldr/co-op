# QUANT

*Play a part loosely, hear it back in time.*

QUANT records what you play and loops it, pulled onto the beat. Pick **QUANT** with Shift + seq, then press seq to open its page.

## Get it

QUANT is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
