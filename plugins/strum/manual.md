# Strum

*A real acoustic guitar that plays along.*

Strum is a Martin HD28 acoustic guitar, recorded note by note. Every key plays the right chord for your key and scale, and Strum's right hand plays it in a pattern that follows the tempo. There are 28 patterns, from campfire strumming and funk to Travis picking, bossa nova and flamenco. Keep it in open chords or move up the neck to barre shapes; the neck on screen shows where every finger goes.

## Who made it

Guitar: Jeff Learman's Martin HD28 recordings from the Discord SFZ GM Bank (CC0).

## Get it

Strum is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
