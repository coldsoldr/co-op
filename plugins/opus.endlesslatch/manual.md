# endless latch

On the endless sequencer's page, tap shift twice and recording stays on as if shift were held, until you press shift again or leave the page. The window for the two taps is a setting (350 ms to start).

## Get it

endless latch 1.0.0 is a plugin for Opus 2.0 beta 4.1, LizardMan's mod for the original OP-1 (firmware 246): one file in the starter pack, or on its own, in [Opus's releases](https://github.com/LizardMan245/opus/releases). Copy the `.opmod` into the mods folder on the OP-1's disk. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy. Opus's plugins are © LizardMan, free to use and share.
