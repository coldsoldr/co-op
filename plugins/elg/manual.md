# Elg

*Slow-moving drones with a low growl.*

Elg is Norwegian for moose. Three saws per voice (the root, a detuned root and a third saw at an interval above) go through a growl drive and a resonant low-pass filter. A slow LFO moves the filter and the level, a little random drift moves the pitch, and every voice moves on its own, so chords breathe and drift apart. On the screen, a moose's head sits under the northern lights. The antlers spread wider with the interval on blue, the breath clouds grow with green, and the moon starts to glow once white goes past about 60 %. The aurora ripples with orange, more while notes play.

## Get it

Elg is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
