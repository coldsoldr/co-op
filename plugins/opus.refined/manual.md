# Refined

Audivit's palette for the whole OP-1, its own screens included (above, on the tape screen): cyan, mint, off-white and red on the encoders, neutral off-white and greys for TE's lavender whites and purple greys, teal and aubergine for the dark blues. The first palette theme: every stock screen, text, the font's coloured icons and every plugin page drawn in stock colours take its colours.

## Get it

Refined 1.0.0 is a plugin for Opus 2.0 beta 5, LizardMan's mod for the original OP-1 (firmware 246): a download of its own beside the starter pack, in [Opus's releases](https://github.com/LizardMan245/opus/releases). Copy the `.opmod` into the mods folder on the OP-1's disk. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy. Opus's plugins are © LizardMan, free to use and share.
