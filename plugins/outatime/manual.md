# outatime

*A time-travel synth that drifts between 1955 and 2015.*

Every voice plays three oscillators, the flux capacitor's three arms. Year picks their wave and Speed lets them drift through the years around it. Power is a strong overdrive, and Trail is the ignition: each note opens a resonant filter that then burns down. The amp envelope on T2, glide and the play modes work as on the other synths. Push blue, green or white for page 2.

## Who made it

outatime is by LizardMan245. Hundekraft ported it to the OP-1.

## Get it

outatime is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
