# SID

*An 8580-inspired chip synth.*

Based on Hermit’s cRSID, SID combines three oscillators per note with a resonant filter. The modes cover triangle, saw, pulse, noise, oscillator sync and ring modulation. It is inspired by the 8580 chip, not an exact copy of one. Use the OP-1's normal ADSR page to shape each note. Turn blue to choose a waveform and white to open or close the filter. The cutoff number shows control position, not hertz.

## Who made it

Adapts Hermit (Mihaly Horvath)'s cRSID 1.58 light renderer.

## Get it

SID is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
