# ENTE

*The pump, without the patch cables.*

ENTE by op1r is a sidechain compressor: it can listen to something other than the sound it turns down. Put it on a synth, pick track 1 as the source, and the synth ducks every time the drums on track 1 hit. It can also listen to the input, or to a steady beat from the tempo and pump in time with no drums at all. You get anything from house-style pumping to a gentle breath under the drums. The rubber duck on screen gets squashed as ENTE turns the sound down, and it lies flat at 24 dB.

## Who made it

From op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

ENTE is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
