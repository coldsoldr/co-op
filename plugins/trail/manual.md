# Trail

*A melody that slowly wanders off.*

Hold a note and Trail plays a short phrase in your scale. Each phrase is built from a short idea of four or eight steps that comes back, changed a little, and the phrase ends on the root or a chord tone. Leave it running and Trail keeps working on that idea: it moves a bit of the tune up a step, turns it upside down or plays it backwards, so the phrase drifts somewhere new while you play over it.

## Get it

Trail is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
