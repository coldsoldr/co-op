# Clouds

*Granular textures, after Mutable Instruments Clouds.*

A port of Mutable Instruments Clouds in granular mode: up to 24 grains over a buffer of about 2.7 seconds. Push an encoder to switch it to its page-2 value.

## Who made it

Port of Mutable Instruments Clouds by Émilie Gillet; from op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Clouds is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
