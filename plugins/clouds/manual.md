# Clouds

*Granular textures, after Mutable Instruments Clouds.*

A port of Mutable Instruments Clouds in granular mode: up to 24 grains over a buffer of about 2.7 seconds. Push an encoder to switch it to its page-2 value.

## Who made it

Port of Mutable Instruments Clouds by Émilie Gillet; from op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Clouds is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.

On op1r 72 it is the plugin file `clouds.op1p`: tick it in [op1r's web patcher](https://pcppcp.github.io/op1r/web/) (Connect OP-1 installs it over USB), or download it here with op1r chosen in Settings.
