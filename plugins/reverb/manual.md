# Reverb

*Reverb, after Mutable Instruments Elements.*

Based on the Elements reverb from Mutable Instruments, drawn as Kali with art by DAN76. Her arms count the decay, the flames show the tone. The skulls and flames change brightness continuously.

## Who made it

After the Mutable Instruments Elements reverb by Émilie Gillet; screen art by DAN76; from op1r.

## Get it

Reverb is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
