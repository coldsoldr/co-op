# Composer

*Hold a key, get a whole song.*

Pick a style and hold a key: Composer writes a whole song, with verse, chorus and bridge and proper chord changes. There are 32 styles, from deep house and drum & bass to bossa nova and film noir, and each brings its own chords, grooves and song form. A song is built from 8-bar sections: A is the verse, B the chorus (the drop in club styles), C the bridge (the break in club styles, funk and nu-disco). Pop runs A A B A B C B B, lo-fi just A A B A. The first time a section comes round it plays as written. After that it changes a little each time, as much as Vary allows. Change the style while it plays and the next bar follows.

## Get it

Composer is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
