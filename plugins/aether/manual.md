# Aether

*A tilt-controlled theremin.*

Hold a key to choose the note, then tilt forward/back to bend its pitch. Raising the right side reduces volume; shaking adds vibrato. The angle at note-on becomes home. The satellite dish follows tilt and the radio waves follow the note envelope. Stock mono, legato and portamento modes still apply.

## Who made it

Satellite artwork by DAN76/Rackes; from op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Aether is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
