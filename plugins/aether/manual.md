# Aether

*A tilt-controlled theremin.*

Hold a key to choose the note, then tilt forward/back to bend its pitch. Raising the right side reduces volume; shaking adds vibrato. The angle at note-on becomes home. The satellite dish follows tilt and the radio waves follow the note envelope. Stock mono, legato and portamento modes still apply.

## Who made it

Satellite artwork by DAN76/Rackes; from op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Aether is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.

On op1r 72 it is the plugin file `aether.op1p`: tick it in [op1r's web patcher](https://pcppcp.github.io/op1r/web/) (Connect OP-1 installs it over USB), or download it here with op1r chosen in Settings.
