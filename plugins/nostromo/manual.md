# Nostromo

*A plate reverb with a clock you can turn down.*

Nostromo by Lewis-Clayton is Jon Dattorro's plate reverb, the design behind the Lexicon 224 and the Deluge's Digital reverb, with the controls of the Chase Bliss CXM 1978. It can be a room, a plate or a hall. The screen draws the reverb's tail like a scope trace, and the ship from *Alien* flies where the tail dies out.

## Who made it

After the Deluge's Digital reverb by Katherine Whitlock and Jon Dattorro's plate; from op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Nostromo is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
