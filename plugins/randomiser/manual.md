# Randomiser

*Keep the instrument. Find another sound inside it.*

On a synth page, hold Shift and press drop to make a new variation of the current sound. The engine stays the same; its knobs, envelope, effect and LFO can change. Randomiser chooses from the effects and LFOs available on this OP-1. MIDI LFO is excluded.

## Who made it

Algorithm, history and panel by Lewis-Clayton / pcppcp; native kennel integration by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Randomiser is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
