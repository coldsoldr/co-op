# Randomiser

*Keep the instrument. Find another sound inside it.*

On a synth page, hold Shift and press drop to make a new variation of the current sound. The engine stays the same; its knobs, envelope, effect and LFO can change. Randomiser chooses from the effects and LFOs available on this OP-1. MIDI LFO is excluded. The RANDOMISER page in Settings picks which of them change, and whether the effect and LFO keep their type.

## Who made it

Algorithm, history and panel by Lewis-Clayton / pcppcp; native kennel integration by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Randomiser is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.

On op1r 73 it is the plugin file `randomiser.op1p`: tick it in [op1r's web patcher](https://pcppcp.github.io/op1r/web/) (Connect OP-1 installs it over USB), or download it here with op1r chosen in Settings.
