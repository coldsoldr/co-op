# Mixtur

*Undertones below every key.*

A subharmonic organ after Oskar Sala's Mixtur-Trautonium. Four dividers play undertones of the key: the key is the top note, and the dividers add notes at 1/n of its frequency below it. Two formant filters turn the result into vowels. It sounds dark, vocal and slightly unearthly. Each divider offers OFF and 1 to 24. The starting values are 1, 2, 3 and 5.

## Who made it

Musica Obscura by ultralazr, from op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Mixtur is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
