# Grids

*A map of drum patterns, after Mutable Instruments Grids.*

Grids plays three parts, BD, SD and HH, from a map of drum patterns. Move the point around the map with X and Y and the patterns morph from one groove into the next. Fill sets how busy each part is, and Chaos adds random variation. It's pcppcp's port of Émilie Gillet's Grids module.

## Who made it

pcppcp's port of Mutable Instruments Grids by Émilie Gillet (GPL-3.0-or-later), ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Grids is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
