# bridge

Runs op1r's synth engines, effects and sequencers (.op1p) and Hundekraft's (.knl) on Opus as they are, with their own screens and pages, and sounds saved with them play. An alpha, a download of its own beside the starter pack.

## Credits

Loads op1r's .op1p files by popcorp and Hundekraft's .knl files; every bridged plugin stays its author's work.

## Get it

bridge 0.10.0 is a plugin for Opus 2.0 beta 4.1, LizardMan's mod for the original OP-1 (firmware 246): a download of its own beside the starter pack, in [Opus's releases](https://github.com/LizardMan245/opus/releases). Copy the `.opmod` into the mods folder on the OP-1's disk. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy. Opus's plugins are © LizardMan, free to use and share.
