# KASTLE

*An FM drone, with a castle on the screen.*

KASTLE is displayname808's castle, rebuilt for the OP-1. Four FM operators make a slow drone. On the screen a castle stands on a small island with its village, and the knobs bring rain, leaves, lightning and fire.

## Who made it

displayname808's castle, rebuilt for the OP-1 by Hundekraft.

## Get it

KASTLE is part of hundekraft 3.3, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
