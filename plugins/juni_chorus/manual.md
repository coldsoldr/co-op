# JUNI chorus

*A stereo ensemble chorus with modes I, II and I+II.*

Made for JUNI, but it works on anything. As the master effect it's stereo, with one delay per side and the two sides moving in opposite directions. On a synth or the drums it's mono. The bass stays dry, so low notes keep their weight.

## Get it

JUNI chorus is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
