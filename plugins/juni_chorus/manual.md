# JUNI chorus

*A stereo ensemble chorus with modes I, II and I+II.*

Made for JUNI, but it works on anything. As the master effect it's stereo, with one delay per side. In I and II the two sides move in opposite directions, which makes it wide; in I+II they move together, as on the original. On a synth or the drums it's mono. The voicing is a Juno-106's, after FM-106 by PatttF and KR-106 by Karl LaRocca.

## Get it

JUNI chorus is part of hundekraft 3.3, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
