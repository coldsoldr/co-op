# GRIP

*A compressor with a HITS kick sidechain.*

A compressor for synths, drums or the master mix, with RMS detection and a program-dependent response after the dbx 160A. Blend the compressed sound back in with Mix, or duck an instrument under the HITS kick.

## Using it

Choose GRIP in a synth, drum or master effect slot. Set threshold, ratio, attack and release, fine-tune the response with knee and range, and use makeup and mix to blend in the compressed sound. With HITS as the drum engine, push white on a synth or drum effect to turn on the kick sidechain. With HITS installed and FLUKE as the drum sequencer, push orange to toggle FLUKE's hold. Turning an encoder faster moves its value further, so long sweeps take fewer turns.

## Who made it

GRIP is by op1lab ([VolkerJunginger/OP-1](https://github.com/VolkerJunginger/OP-1), GPL-3.0), published by ESC_CODE with FLUKE, HITS and FLUX. Its RMS detection and gain response draw on the dbx 160A. Built with LizardMan's Opus plugin kit.

## Get it

FLUKE, HITS, FLUX and GRIP come as a set from the [device page](https://volkerjunginger.github.io/OP-1/), one `.opmod` each or all four in a zip, for Opus core 2.57 or later on the original OP-1 (firmware 246). Copy the files to the `mods` folder on the OP-1's disk, eject and restart; replace older copies by file name and remove an old `hitsfluke.opmod`. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
