# sim

sim is a synthesized drum kit by LizardMan. Pick it as the drum type: press drum, hold Shift and press T1. Every hit throws a solid into a glass box on the screen. Low sounds make big ones, long decays make them bounce more, and metal sounds turn sharp-edged.

## Get it

sim is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
