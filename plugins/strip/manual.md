# Channel strip

*A high-pass, a low-pass, an EQ band, drive and level on the synth, the drums and the tape tracks.*

The channel strip shapes the synth and the drums before the tape, like the channel on a mixing desk. It sits before their effect. There are two strips, one for the synth and one for the drums. They stay as they are when you change sounds or kits, and they are kept after a restart. Both start switched off, so nothing changes until you turn one on: hold Shift and turn green right.

## Get it

Channel strip is part of hundekraft 3.3, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
