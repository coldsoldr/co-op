# matrix

An LFO matrix in the OP-1's own LFO list. Up to eight rows on one page, each an LFO (sine, triangle, saw up or down, square, sample and hold; 0.1 to 50 Hz, or a note length from 4 bars to 1/32 synced to the tempo) or an envelope per note, with an amount and a destination: the engine's knobs and their shift layer, the amp envelope, the effect's knobs, the sound's volume, pitch, ring or duck, or another row's amount. Its settings are saved with the sound.

## Get it

matrix 0.5.1 is a plugin for Opus 2.0 beta 4.1, LizardMan's mod for the original OP-1 (firmware 246): one file in the starter pack, or on its own, in [Opus's releases](https://github.com/LizardMan245/opus/releases). Copy the `.opmod` into the mods folder on the OP-1's disk. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy. Opus's plugins are © LizardMan, free to use and share.
