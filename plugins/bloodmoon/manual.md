# Bloodmoon

*A dark organ with a haunted bell.*

Bloodmoon by Nola Drift (SammiBabe666) is a gothic organ. Every note is a pipe with its 2nd, 3rd and 4th harmonics, and a bell strikes on top of it. The bell's two partials sit a little off the organ, close to a minor third, so it never quite agrees with the pipes. A slow vibrato fades in after the attack, with a faint wobble under it, so held notes drift like old pipes.

## Who made it

It's from Project Bloodmoon by Nola Drift, published by SammiBabe666. Hundekraft brought it to the OP-1.

## Get it

Bloodmoon is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
