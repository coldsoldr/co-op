# Bloodmoon

*A dark organ with a haunted bell.*

Bloodmoon by Nola Drift is a gothic organ with a haunted bell. Every note is a pipe with its 2nd, 3rd and 4th harmonics, and a bell strikes on top of it, a little off the organ, close to a minor third. A slow vibrato fades in after the attack with a faint wobble under it, so held notes drift like old pipes. On screen, a black cathedral under a blood-red moon moves with every knob.

## Controls

| Encoder | Name | What it does | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blue | Blood | Harmonic richness: hollow and thin at the bottom, thick and dark at the top | The rose window turns |
| Green | Eclipse | The bell: how loud it strikes and how long it rings. Fully down, no bell | The tower bells swing |
| White | Haunting | Vibrato depth (up to about ±73 cents, after the attack) and a steady pitch drift | The stars flicker |
| Orange | Moonrise | Vibrato and wobble speed, from about 0.15 Hz to 9 Hz, mostly slow | The moon rises |

Shape the attack and release with the OP-1's own envelope on T2. The repo has eight presets (cathedral, funeral bell, crypt drone and five more) for the hundekraft build.

## Who made it

Bloodmoon is by Nola Drift ([Project_Bloodmoon](https://github.com/SammiBabe666/Project_Bloodmoon), GPL-3.0-or-later with the OP-1 firmware permission), published by SammiBabe666. It's also part of hundekraft's workshop.

## Get it

There are two builds for the original OP-1 (firmware 246); install only one.

- **hundekraft:** bloodmoon.knl 1.4, for hundekraft 3.0 or newer. Install over USB from its card here (co-op fetches the file from the author's repo and checks it), add it under Your plugins in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/), or tick it there, where it comes with hundekraft 3.2.6.
- **opus:** bloodmoon.opmod 1.5.2, for Opus core 2.6 or newer. In disk mode, copy it into the OP-1's `mods` folder and restart; the mods list should say it's loaded.

The author notes that neither build has been confirmed on a real OP-1 yet (the op1r build 1.3 has), so back up first and start with the volume low. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
