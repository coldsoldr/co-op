# DRIFT

*A neural net in place of an oscillator.*

DRIFT has no oscillator in the usual sense. A small neural net, trained on real instruments and classic synths, draws every waveform while you play, hundreds of times a second, right on the OP-1. The four encoders don't set a filter or an envelope. They move you through what the net learned, so one turn can bend a flute towards a bell, or land somewhere between a choir and a bass that nobody ever recorded. Each note keeps changing while it sounds, and how hard and how high you play changes it too.

## Get it

DRIFT is part of hundekraft 3.3, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
