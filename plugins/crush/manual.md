# Crush

*Bit depth, sample rate and drive, after Maiko.*

Crush reduces the signal's bit depth and sample rate, then adds drive. Low bit depth makes a waveform's steps audible; a low sample rate adds aliasing. The artwork is Maiko's, used with permission. It runs on synth, drum and master FX; the master keeps the two channels separate.

## Who made it

Reconstructs Maiko Pruett's #250 algorithm and artwork, used with permission.

## Get it

Crush is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
