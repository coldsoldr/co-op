# Snowglobe

*Shimmer reverb with a frozen heart.*

A shimmer reverb whose tail climbs an octave at a time. At the top of orange the tail freezes into a pad that holds until you turn back.

## Get it

Snowglobe is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
