# PINBOARD

*A synth you patch with pins.*

PINBOARD is a synth built around a pin board, inspired by the EMS VCS3: its pin matrix, its diode-ladder filter, its ring modulator and its trapezoid envelope. It has two oscillators, a noise source, a ring modulator and a filter that squeals when you push it. A third oscillator runs slow or fast and is there to modulate the others. The board is a grid of eight sources and eight destinations. Put a pin where a source crosses a destination and that source moves it: osc 3 wobbles the filter, the envelope sweeps the pitch, the tilt of the OP-1 bends a note. Each voice has its own filter, so chords stay clean.

## Get it

PINBOARD is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
