# SIM

A drum engine whose page is a physics simulation: every hit throws a solid into a virtual box, and what it does shows the key's sound. Its size is the pitch, its bounce the decay, its throw the sweep, its debris the noise, and a scope along the top draws the sound. It follows the endless sequencer's transposition and your colour theme. SIM draws with orbit3d, which goes in mods beside it.

## Get it

SIM 0.1.9 is a plugin for Opus 2.0 beta 4.1, LizardMan's mod for the original OP-1 (firmware 246): one file in the starter pack, or on its own, in [Opus's releases](https://github.com/LizardMan245/opus/releases). Copy the `.opmod` into the mods folder on the OP-1's disk. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy. Opus's plugins are © LizardMan, free to use and share.
