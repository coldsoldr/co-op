# HIM

*Drums that bounce.*

HIM is a synthesized drum kit by LizardMan. Every hit throws a solid into a glass box on the screen: low sounds are big, long decays bounce more, and metal sounds turn into sharp shapes. Pick HIM as the drum type. The drum preset list has four HIM kits.

## Get it

HIM is part of hundekraft 3.3, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
