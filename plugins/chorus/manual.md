# Chorus

chorus, by LizardMan, has one to three voices and a triangle or sine sweep, and it's stereo as the master effect. On screen each voice is a stack of waves in its encoder's colour.

## Get it

Chorus is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
