# Windwood

*A clarinet, built from the reed up.*

Windwood is nvmsocool's clarinet. There are no recordings in it: a reed, the air flowing past it and the tube are simulated sample by sample, and the numbers were fitted to recordings of a real clarinet. Soft notes are close to a sine and slow to speak. Loud ones are bright and reedy.

## Get it

Windwood is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.

On op1r 73 it is the plugin file `windwood.op1p`: tick it in [op1r's web patcher](https://pcppcp.github.io/op1r/web/) (Connect OP-1 installs it over USB), or download it here with op1r chosen in Settings.
