# rackverb

*The late-80s studio rack, in one effect.*

rackverb is a rack of four effects units from the late 80s, with 316 programs between them: big halls, gated drum rooms and reverse reverbs, then flangers, slapback echoes and wide choruses. VERB I has 63 programs: reverbs, gated rooms and reverse rooms. VERB II has 100, from 00 to 99: reverbs, gates, reverses, flangers, choruses, delays and a few odd ones. Program 00 is a bypass. FEX has 63 echoes, multi-tap delays, slapbacks and stereo wideners. QUAD is a multi-effect with 90 programs built from EQ, chorus, flanger, phaser, a rotating speaker, delay and reverb. Pick a unit, turn orange one click per program, and read the program's name under the number.

## Who made it

Written from Eric Brombaugh's and thement's published Midiverb research; no Alesis ROM data.

## Get it

rackverb is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
