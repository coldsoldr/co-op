# Oblique strategies

Shift + help shows the clock or a random card from Brian Eno and Peter Schmidt's Oblique Strategies, whichever you looked at last. Press Shift + help again to switch between them. A new card turns up each time you switch to it.

## Who made it

Cards by Brian Eno and Peter Schmidt; idea and card screen from ultralazr's op1-oblique-strategies.

## Get it

Oblique strategies is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
