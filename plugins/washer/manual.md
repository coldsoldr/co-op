# Washer

*Chorus, ensemble and flanger.*

A chorus that turns into a flanger. Three taps of one short delay line are swept by an LFO a third of a turn apart, which gives the wide ensemble sound of old string machines. Add foam and the taps move closer and feed back until it's a jet flanger.

## Get it

Washer is part of hundekraft 3.3.1, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.

On op1r 73 it is the plugin file `washer.op1p`: tick it in [op1r's web patcher](https://pcppcp.github.io/op1r/web/) (Connect OP-1 installs it over USB), or download it here with op1r chosen in Settings.
