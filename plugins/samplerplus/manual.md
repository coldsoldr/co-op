# Sampler+

*The synth sampler with envelopes, filtering and loop controls.*

Choose **sampler+** in the synth type browser (Shift + T1). A sampler slot keeps its recording; from another engine it becomes a sampler with whatever sample that slot holds. It stays a normal sampler sound, with sampler+'s settings saved alongside. Without sampler+ in your build the recording plays through the stock sampler; its extras come back when you install it again.

## Who made it

From op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Sampler+ is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
