# Punch in

Punch-in uses tape markers. Hold tape and press REC to arm it: the markers turn red. Play the tape. Recording starts at the next marker and stops at the one after. Press STOP to stop, or tape + REC again to disarm.

## Who made it

From op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Punch in is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
