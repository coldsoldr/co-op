# Supersaw

*Detuned saw stack.*

Two detuned saws with a sub oscillator and drive. The trance lead and the hoover live here. The screen is a chainsaw: the chain teeth draw the waveform and the saw shakes with drive.

## Who made it

From op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Supersaw is part of hundekraft 3.3.2, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.

On op1r 77 it is the plugin file `supersaw.op1p`: tick it in [op1r's web patcher](https://pcppcp.github.io/op1r/web/) (Connect OP-1 installs it over USB), or download it here with op1r chosen in Settings.
