# Supersaw

*Detuned saw stack.*

Two detuned saws with a sub oscillator and drive. The trance lead and the hoover live here. The screen is a chainsaw: the chain teeth draw the waveform and the saw shakes with drive.

## Who made it

From op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

Supersaw is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
