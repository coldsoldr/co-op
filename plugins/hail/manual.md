# HAIL

*Clouds of grains, after Mutable Instruments Beads.*

HAIL records what comes in and plays many short, overlapping grains from it: pitched, reversed, smeared and sent into a reverb. It's based on the granular mode of Mutable Instruments Beads, with a buffer of about 1.5 seconds and up to 12 grains at once (8 while the reverb is on).

## Who made it

Port of Mutable Instruments Beads by Émilie Gillet (MIT).

## Get it

HAIL is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
