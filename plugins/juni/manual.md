# JUNI

*An early-80s polysynth with its own chorus.*

Each voice has one oscillator with a saw, a pulse and a sub an octave down, into a four-pole filter. Every knob moves several settings at once, so most positions sound good. The T2 envelope shapes both the volume and the filter, as it did on the originals. Push blue, green or white for page 2.

## Who made it

The filter and the chorus are voiced like a Juno-106, after FM-106 by PatttF, which builds on KR-106 by Karl LaRocca (GPL-3.0, used with permission).

## Get it

JUNI is part of hundekraft 3.3.2, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.

On op1r 77 it is the plugin file `juni.op1p`: tick it in [op1r's web patcher](https://pcppcp.github.io/op1r/web/) (Connect OP-1 installs it over USB), or download it here with op1r chosen in Settings.
