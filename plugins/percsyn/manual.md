# Percsyn

A PO-32 / Microtonic style drum synth, in two plugins. percsyn is a synth engine (one sound across the keyboard, 10 built-in sounds and 16 places for PO-32 transfers recorded into the slot's sample). perc is a drum type: a kit of 24 sounds, one per key; the pages edit the sound itself (T1 tone, T2 noise, T4 mix; Shift + turn the second layer), Shift + blue browses presets and transferred sounds. Patch model after libpo32 (MIT, Eric Lewis)

## Who made it

PO-32 patch model after libpo32 by Eric Lewis (MIT); in op1r by pcppcp.

## Get it

Percsyn is a plugin of op1r 72, pcppcp's custom firmware for the original OP-1 (R246): the file `percsyn.op1p`. Tick it in [op1r's web patcher](https://pcppcp.github.io/op1r/web/) (Connect OP-1 installs it over USB), or download it here and send it with the patcher. co-op hosts no copy: the download comes from op1r's repo, checked against its hash.
