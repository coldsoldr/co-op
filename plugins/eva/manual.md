# EVA

*An astronaut's choir over the helmet radio.*

A warm, sung pad with the sound of early-80s film scores. Two slightly detuned oscillators, a saw and a soft pulse, go through a lowpass with a gentle vowel colour on top, then through a wide ensemble chorus. Each voice has a slow vibrato and drifts a little on its own, so held chords slowly shift. The helmet radio sits on Com: low settings just warm the sound up, high settings bring in hiss, dropouts and the radio's beeps.

## Get it

EVA is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
