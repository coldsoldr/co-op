# VOX

*Your voice, played on the keys.*

Sing or talk into the OP-1, hold a chord, and VOX puts your words on those notes. The keys play a synth sound and your voice shapes it, so the chord you hold talks or sings. VOX takes your voice from the OP-1's input, like Amp: the mic, line, radio or USB. It splits your voice into up to 24 bands, and each band opens the same band of the synth. Chord mode, scale mode, the sequencers and MIDI all play the keys as usual.

## Get it

VOX is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
