# HOLD

*Hold keys, get a loop.*

HOLD turns the keys you hold into a short loop. While it runs, every key you hold is written into the step under the playhead: keys held together make a chord, one after another a line. A key held across several steps becomes one long note. Let go and the pattern keeps looping. Hold a chord through a whole pass and you get a drone. It doesn't retrigger, even where the loop starts over. The rings on screen fill in as you write.

## Get it

HOLD is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
