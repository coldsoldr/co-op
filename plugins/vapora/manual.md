# VAPORA

*Six voices of drifting pads.*

VAPORA by AiAAAA (from op1r) is a pad synth. Every voice plays two slowly drifting, detuned wave heads from fourteen Characters, from soft choirs to glassy bells. The screen is a sky: waves for the sound, a star field for the second head, and a sun that rises with the brightness.

## Who made it

By AiAAAA and the VAPORA contributors; from op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

VAPORA is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
