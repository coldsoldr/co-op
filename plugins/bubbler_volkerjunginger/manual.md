# Bubbler

*Granular delay with overlapping grains, variable timing, pitch and reverse playback*

Granular delay with overlapping grains, variable timing, pitch and reverse playback.

## Who made it

Bubbler is by ESC_CODE ([VolkerJunginger/OP-1](https://github.com/VolkerJunginger/OP-1), GPL-3.0-only).

## Get it

It needs opus 2.31, on the original OP-1. Download it from [its page](https://github.com/VolkerJunginger/OP-1/tree/main/Delay-Trio) and follow the install steps there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
