# USB out

Shift + help, then T2. Turn blue to choose MASTER or MASTER + TRACKS.

USB always has six channels: main left and right on 1 and 2, and the four tape tracks on 3 to 6. With MASTER the track channels are silent. With MASTER + TRACKS they carry the four tape tracks, taken before the faders. MASTER (the default) uses less processing. On Windows, remove the old OP-1 audio device if it still shows two channels.

## Who made it

From op1r, ported to kennel by Hundekraft.

## Get it

USB out is part of hundekraft 3.2.6, a custom firmware for the original OP-1. Tick it in the [hundekraft workshop](https://hundekraft.github.io/op1-workshop/) and install it from there. co-op lists it for reference and hosts no copy.
